Are all REKOM hoodies handmade?

Yes. Every hoodie is handcrafted in our personal workshop in Reus (Spain). We cut, print and finish each piece ourselves to keep full control over quality, fit and details. No mass production, no shortcuts.

Are your drops limited?

Yes. REKOM is built around limited runs. Once a hoodie is gone, in most cases it will not be restocked. This keeps each piece more exclusive and aligned with our underground mindset.

How do I choose my size?

Our hoodies are based on a relaxed, urban fit. We usually group sizes as S–M and L–XL:

  • S–M → recommended if you prefer a more regular or slightly loose fit.
  • L–XL → ideal if you like it oversized or have a bigger build.

If you're between two options, go for the bigger one for a more streetwear look.

Where do you ship from and to?

All orders ship from Spain. The available countries, delivery fees and estimated times are detailed in our Shipping & Returns section.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Delivery time depends on your destination and the shipping method available at checkout. Once shipped, you'll receive tracking information whenever possible. Keep in mind that external factors (couriers, customs, holidays) may affect delivery times.

Can I return or exchange a hoodie?

Yes, as long as it complies with the conditions in our Returns & Exchanges Policy: unworn, in perfect condition and within the stated time frame.

How should I care for my REKOM hoodie?

To keep it sharp for as long as possible:

  • Wash inside out, cold or max 30ºC.
  • Avoid tumble drying when you can.
  • Do not iron directly over prints.

Treat it like a long-term piece, not a disposable garment.

Will sold-out pieces be restocked?

Usually not. Most REKOM drops are designed as limited runs. If we ever bring a design back, it will likely be reworked, updated or released in a different version — not a simple reprint.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept the secure payment methods available at checkout (such as major debit/credit cards and other digital payments, depending on your region). All payments are processed through trusted providers using encrypted connections.

I still have questions. How can I contact you?

You can reach us through the form on our Contact page or via the channels listed there. We’ll get back to you as soon as possible.
